About Calic Consulting

Calic Consulting is a boutique advisory firm built around a single conviction: that complex organisations deserve access to exceptional talent and deep expertise — delivered with commitment, agility, and without the overhead of large consultancy firms.

Founded by Altaf Daya, a chartered accountant with experience spanning KPMG, Accenture, and senior roles in the energy sector, Calic brings together a carefully selected network of finance specialists spanning reporting, planning, transformation, and commercial disciplines.

We work alongside organisations in the public, private, and non-profit sectors to strengthen financial capability, deliver complex finance transformation, and build lasting resilience. Whether it's modernising a finance function, leading a finance systems programme, or developing a business case that withstands scrutiny, we bring the same rigour, pragmatism, and commitment to every engagement.
